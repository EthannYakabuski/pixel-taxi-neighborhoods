Pixel Taxi Neighborhoods
Drive your own 3D taxi, follow the GPS and earn tips across three neighborhoods.

Take a yellow taxi through a connected 3D city of factory streets, downtown boulevards and leafy neighborhoods. Real turns, curved roads and a camera behind your cab make every pickup a small driving journey.

THREE SELLING CARDS
You drive. GPS guides.
Follow a gold route through68 connected curved streets across Foundry, Central and Garden. Wrong turns update the directions, while your hands stay on the controls.

One offer, one passenger
Accept or decline a dispatch, stop at the curb and earn a tip for a prompt, collision-free ride.

A cab and career of your own
Keep your fares, improve your taxi, meet rare guests and build a journal across many small shifts.
