Pixel Taxi Neighborhoods
A little city. A hundred stories to bring home.

Pixel Taxi Neighborhoods is a friendly top-down driving game set in a little city with three distinct districts. Choose a passenger job, find the pickup curb, and follow the connected streets to the destination. There is no need to turn every journey into a race.

THREE SELLING CARDS
Three neighborhoods, one connected city
Find your way through the Foundry, Central and Garden districts, with a new friendly fare around the corner.

Every passenger has a destination
Choose a job, reach the pickup curb, and bring your passenger home. Unusual guests make a familiar route feel new.

Make this cab yours
Grow your driver level, improve the engine, comfort and radio, and pick a color that feels like your taxi.

TECHNICAL INFORMATION
Made with Godot 4.5.1 Compatibility. Portrait Android is primary; a Web build is provided where feasible. Local saves support offline core play. Android services have graceful Web fallbacks. Original code is Apache-2.0; source-asset/plugin notices are preserved. Google Play Games and production ads require the owner's final configuration.
