Pixel Taxi Neighborhoods

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80/80 characters)
Drive your own 3D taxi, follow the GPS and earn tips across three neighborhoods.

LONG DESCRIPTION
Every fare has a story. You are at the wheel.

Take a yellow taxi through a connected 3D city of factory streets, downtown boulevards and leafy neighborhoods. Real turns, curved roads and a camera behind your cab make every pickup a small driving journey.

Dispatch sends one offer at a time. Accept it or wait for the next passenger, then drive to the gold curb. Hold and drag with one thumb to accelerate and steer, or use the separate steering, brake and reverse controls. GPS marks the shortest connected road route and recalculates when you miss a turn. You do the driving.

Bring your passenger home promptly and without a bump to earn a clean-ride tip. Collisions slow the taxi and affect the tip, while the normal fare and your banked coins remain safe. Take a relaxed route when you prefer; there is no need to lose your progress over a difficult corner.

Improve the engine, comfort and radio, choose from four cars and grow your driver level. Meet rare guests, fill your passenger journal and return for daily goals and a seven-day gift cycle.

Core driving works offline with local saves. Android includes optional rewarded fare bonuses and ads between completed fares. Pick up the next story and make the neighborhood your own.
